Poker Guide: ICM Basics For Mid Stakes

Mid-stakes ICM framework for payout-aware risk, stack leverage, and better final-third tournament decisions.

Use with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

ICM foundation for real tables

ICM converts chips into payout equity; equal-chip plays are often unequal-money plays.

  • Tighten marginal calls when busting hurts ladder EV.
  • Widen pressure lines when opponents are payout-protecting.
  • Recalculate ranges every pay-jump tier.

Calling vs jamming under ICM

Most ICM punts come from bad calls, not bad jams.

  • Call tighter versus covering stacks.
  • Jam aggressively when fold equity plus ladder pressure is high.
  • Avoid dominated calls in medium-stack survival nodes.

Practical mid-stakes leaks

  • Using chip-EV charts deep into pay-jump zones.
  • Ignoring relative stacks and focusing only on hand strength.
  • Failing to tag ICM spots for dedicated review.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag 5 spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction.
  • Week 3: Stress-test correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on data.
