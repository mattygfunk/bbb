Poker Guide: Opening Ranges By Position

Practical positional opening framework: tighter early, wider late, and stack-depth adjustments that hold up in tournaments.

Pair with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Baseline by seat

Position determines profitability before cards do.

  • Early position: strongest linear opens, low domination risk.
  • Middle: add suited broadways and selective wheel combos.
  • Late: widest opens, but trim versus aggressive blinds.

Stack-depth overlays

Same hand can be open/fold based on effective stack.

  • Deep: prioritize postflop playability and nut potential.
  • 25–40bb: reduce marginal offsuit opens out of position.
  • Short: convert weak opens into push/fold simplifications.

Population-aware tuning

Default ranges are starting points, not religion.

  • Widen late opens against over-folding blinds.
  • Tighten opens where blinds 3-bet aggressively.
  • Keep adjustment notes by table type to avoid guesswork.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag five spots.
  • Week 2: Review and define one correction rule.
  • Week 3: Stress-test rule under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on outcomes.
