How Poker Nights Work

Big Bluff Bay runs structured no-limit hold'em tournaments with posted blind levels, start times, and event conditions (see Poker Rules).

Core schedule: Sunday 6 PM Main Event, nightly 6 PM and 10 PM GTD tournaments. This structure suits players looking for Melbourne poker tournaments and players comparing Sydney cash games with scheduled tournament play. Some majors include premium prizes (full times on the Poker Schedule page).

Fair play is mandatory: no collusion, no abuse, and no angle-shooting. Admin moderation protects game integrity, with promo details covered in Promo Terms.
