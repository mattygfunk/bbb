Poker Guide: Tilt Control And Recovery

Operational tilt system: pre-commit triggers, in-session reset protocol, and post-session recovery routines.

Pair with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Pre-session anti-tilt setup

Tilt prevention starts before first hand.

  • Define three personal tilt triggers in writing.
  • Set hard stop-loss and re-entry rules pre-session.
  • Prepare a 3-minute reset routine you can execute instantly.

In-session recovery protocol

When tilt appears, act fast and mechanical.

  • Stop registration immediately after emotional spike.
  • Run a timed reset: breathe, hydrate, short walk, review objective.
  • Restart only if decision quality returns above threshold.

Post-session rebuild

Recovery protects tomorrow’s edge.

  • Classify errors: technical vs emotional vs variance.
  • Extract one prevention rule from each tilt incident.
  • Lower stakes/volume briefly if confidence is unstable.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag five spots.
  • Week 2: Review and define one correction rule.
  • Week 3: Stress-test rule under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on outcomes.
