Poker Guide: Tilt Control and Recovery

Apply this guide with your weekly event plan for better consistency and stronger decision quality.

Tilt reality

Tilt is not a personality flaw; it is a state-management issue. Treat it as a protocol problem.

Early warning signs

  • Speed-clicking decisions
  • Breaking your planned buy-in limits
  • Forcing bluffs to “get even”

Recovery protocol

  1. Stop registration for 10 minutes.
  2. Write the exact trigger event.
  3. Resume only if you can follow your pre-set rules.

Guardrails that work

  • Session cap per night
  • Hard bankroll limits
  • One-click cooldown rule after bad beats

Post-tilt improvement

Review tilt spots separately from technical hands so you can fix root causes faster.
