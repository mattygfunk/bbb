Poker Guide: Note Taking That Actually Helps

Deep practical guide to note taking that actually helps for Big Bluff Bay players with clear execution frameworks, in-session checklists, and improvement loops.

Pair this with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Environment adaptation

Adapt strategy to game speed, player pool, and information quality in your current environment.

  • Use population priors online; use baseline deviations live.
  • Adjust pace and time-bank use to high-EV nodes only.
  • Continuously re-rate table quality and seat EV.

Execution checklist

  • What is the biggest environment edge available right now?
  • Am I overloading volume beyond quality threshold?
  • What note today will save chips next week?

Common leak patterns

  • Autopilot decisions under time pressure.
  • Over-adjusting from tiny samples.
  • No post-session review loop.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply this framework in two sessions.
  • Week 2: Tag and review five relevant spots.
  • Week 3: Apply one correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep, refine, or replace based on evidence.
