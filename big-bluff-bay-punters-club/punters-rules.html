Punters Club Rules (Official)

These rules govern weekly punters rounds, scoring outcomes, and jackpot mechanics unless a round-specific notice says otherwise.

1) Lock Time and Submission

  • Tips can be edited until lock time.
  • After lock, picks are frozen for the round.
  • Players are responsible for checking saved picks pre-lock.

2) Scoring

  • 1st place pick = 3 points
  • 2nd place pick = 2 points
  • 3rd place pick = 1 point
  • Unplaced = 0 points

3) Winner and Tie-Breaks

  • Highest total points wins the round.
  • Tie-break uses winners/firsts logic where configured.
  • If no outright winner under published logic, rollover rules apply.

4) Jackpot and Paid Status

  • Current pot is based on paid eligible entries and round settings.
  • Rollover carries if no outright qualifying winner is declared.
  • Paid-status eligibility may affect jackpot outcomes per current settings.

5) Admin Corrections and Disputes

  • Admin may correct official results and obvious data errors.
  • Disputes should be raised promptly with race/member detail.
  • Admin ruling is final for the round.
