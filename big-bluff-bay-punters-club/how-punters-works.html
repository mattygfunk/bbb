How Punters Club Works

Submit picks before lock, score from official race results, and compete across weekly rounds with transparent standings. The format rewards disciplined decisions over random variance.

Round structure

  • Enter or edit tips until lock time.
  • Score through the 3/2/1 model (1st/2nd/3rd).
  • Track results in leaderboard + winner history.
  • If no outright winner under configured logic, rollover may apply.

See Punters Rules and Punters FAQ for full detail.

Consistency model

Strong punters usually focus on race quality, not quantity. Pick spots where your read is strongest, avoid rushed late edits, and review your process each week. This lifts floor performance and compounds over time.

  • Pre-lock: finalise with enough time to check selections.
  • Post-round: review misses by category (map, pace, price, overconfidence).
  • Next week: reduce avoidable errors, not just chase bigger outcomes.
