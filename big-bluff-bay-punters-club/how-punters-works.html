How Punters Club Works

Submit picks before lock, score points from official results, and compete for weekly standings and rollover-aware jackpot outcomes.

Round structure

  • Submit/edit tips until lock time.
  • Score via 3/2/1 system for 1st/2nd/3rd.
  • Track progress in live leaderboard and winners history.

See Punters Rules and Punters FAQ for edge cases.

Weekly edge model

  1. Prioritise races where your read is strongest.
  2. Lock entries early to avoid rushed edits.
  3. Review outcome quality, not just final points.
  4. Build consistency over hero-shot variance.
