How Punters Club Works

Submit picks before lock. Score 3/2/1 points for 1st/2nd/3rd. Highest total wins the week. If no outright winner, jackpot rolls over.

Consistency matters: build points over time, track streak milestones, and avoid rushed low-confidence picks before lock.
