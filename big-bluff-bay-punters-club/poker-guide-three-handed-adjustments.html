Poker Guide: Three Handed Adjustments

Three-handed tournament strategy for wider ranges, positional pressure, and endgame stability.

Use with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Range widening with discipline

  • Open wider from button and small blind.
  • Defend blinds with more playable combos.
  • Keep offsuit trash under control despite short-handed pressure.

Aggression map

  • Apply frequent pressure to capped check/call lines.
  • 3-bet selectively versus over-opening opponents.
  • Use turn sizing to deny equity and simplify river nodes.

Three-handed leaks

  • Playing full-ring ranges and getting run over.
  • Over-bluffing because action feels fast.
  • Ignoring stack-depth transitions between orbits.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag 5 spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction.
  • Week 3: Stress-test correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on data.
