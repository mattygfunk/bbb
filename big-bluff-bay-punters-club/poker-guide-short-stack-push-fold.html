Poker Guide: Short-Stack Push/Fold Framework

Use this guide with your weekly plan from Poker Schedule and policy clarity from Poker FAQ.

Push/fold basics

  • Use stack depth + position first, hand strength second.
  • Prefer shoving over flatting when fold equity is material.
  • Call ranges must tighten when pay-jump pressure is high.

Common mistakes

Calling too wide versus late-position jams and passing profitable reshove spots.
