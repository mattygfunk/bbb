Poker Guide: Short Stack Push Fold

Short-stack tournament guide for profitable jam/fold decisions and avoiding dominated bust-outs.

Pair with Poker FAQ and Poker Rules.

Push-fold fundamentals

At short depth, simplicity often beats creativity.

  • Prioritize fold equity and blockers in jam candidates.
  • Avoid flat-calls that leave awkward SPR postflop.
  • Know position-based jam thresholds by effective blinds.

Reshove discipline

Reshoves must target wide opens, not strong ranges.

  • Attack late opens from players opening too wide.
  • Pass marginal reshoves versus tight EP opens.
  • Use ICM pressure to tighten calling ranges.

Common short-stack mistakes

Most bust-outs are preventable.

  • Waiting too long and losing fold equity.
  • Calling off dominated hands instead of jamming first.
  • Ignoring pay-jump dynamics when near money/final table.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag five spots.
  • Week 2: Review and define one correction rule.
  • Week 3: Stress-test rule under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on outcomes.
