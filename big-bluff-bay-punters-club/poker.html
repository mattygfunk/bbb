What the club is

Big Bluff Bay Poker Club is a social tournament-style poker group. We run structured nights with clear start times, agreed buy-ins, and respectful table culture.

Freeroll feature: when the Bosses Multi lands, that week’s Punters Club players receive a next-day Poker freeroll for the full amount.

  • Tournament format: No-limit hold’em with blind levels.
  • Community first: Friendly table, competitive mindset.
  • Consistent structure: Same rules each week.

Poker FAQ

1) Do I need to be in Punters Club to join poker nights?

It helps. Register in Punters Club first so we can verify player details and eligibility for linked promos.

2) What game format is used?

No-limit Texas Hold’em tournament format with structured blind levels.

3) What time is the Sunday Main Event?

Every Sunday at 6:00 PM with a $1,300 GTD prize pool.

4) What runs nightly at 6:00 PM?

A $500 GTD event plus a Main Event ticket add-on.

5) What runs nightly at 10:00 PM?

A $700 GTD nightly event.

6) Are there special major events?

Yes. Some bigger events include premium first-place prizes, including holiday packages.

7) Was there a recent major-event winner?

Yes — Sylvia won the last major event week.

8) Can beginners play?

Absolutely. New players are welcome if they follow the posted structure and table etiquette.

9) Is this cash game or tournament play?

Tournament play, not an open-ended cash table.

10) How do I register for a tournament night?

Use the Join section on this page, then follow the admin instructions for seat confirmation.

11) Can I join late?

Late registration depends on the event rules for that night.

12) Are re-entries or rebuys allowed?

Only where specifically listed for that event.

13) How many players are usually in each event?

Field size varies by night and schedule, but structure stays consistent.

14) What does GTD mean?

GTD means Guaranteed Prize Pool — the minimum prize amount for that event.

15) What if entries exceed expectations?

Prize pools may increase above the guaranteed amount based on event settings.

16) Are payouts winner-take-all?

No. Payouts are distributed across finishing places under the event’s payout structure.

17) How are seats allocated?

Seats are allocated by host/admin confirmation and event capacity.

18) Is there a waiting list if full?

Yes, for high-demand nights you may be waitlisted and notified if a seat opens.

19) How do table balances work?

Tables are balanced as needed to keep gameplay fair and moving.

20) Are blind levels fixed?

Each event has a published blind structure before play starts.

21) Are breaks scheduled?

Yes, breaks are scheduled by tournament stage and field size.

22) What happens if I disconnect or miss action?

Standard tournament rules apply; repeated missed action may result in penalties or removal.

23) Can I transfer my seat to someone else?

No seat transfers unless approved by admin before event start.

24) Are collusion and chip-dumping allowed?

Never. Any collusion, soft play, or chip-dumping can lead to disqualification.

25) Is abusive chat tolerated?

No. Respectful table behaviour is mandatory.

26) Can decisions be reviewed?

Yes. Admin decisions can be reviewed with hand/timing context where available.

27) What if there is a tie on final hands?

Standard poker tie resolution applies according to hand rankings and board runout.

28) How is the Sunday Main ticket awarded from nightly events?

Ticket conditions are posted in each nightly event announcement.

29) Can I win multiple Main Event tickets?

Depends on that event’s ticket policy; details are posted before registration opens.

30) Do Punters Club players get poker bonuses?

Yes. If the Bosses Multi lands, eligible weekly Punters Club players receive a next-day poker freeroll.

31) How do freerolls work?

Freerolls are no-buy-in promotional events for eligible players under posted conditions.

32) Where are tournament updates posted?

Updates are posted via site announcements and club channels.

33) Can I suggest a new tournament format?

Yes. Player feedback is welcome and reviewed by hosts.

34) Are bounty events planned?

Occasionally. Special format nights are announced in advance.

35) Can I play on mobile?

Site info is mobile-friendly; gameplay format details are provided per event.

36) Is there a player leaderboard?

Leaderboard and winner highlights are published through club updates and page content.

37) How often are schedules updated?

Schedules are updated as needed, with weekly cadence for regular events.

38) What should I do before my first event?

Register early, confirm seat, read rules, and arrive before start time.

39) Who do I contact for support?

Use the Contact Admin link in the Join section for help with access or event questions.

40) Why “Punters legends to poker legends”?

Because the same discipline wins both games: patience, timing, and pressure decisions.
