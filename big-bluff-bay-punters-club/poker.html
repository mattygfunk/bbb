What the club is

Big Bluff Bay Poker Club is a social tournament-style poker group. We run structured nights with clear start times, agreed buy-ins, and respectful table culture (see Poker Rules and How Poker Works).

Freeroll feature: when the Bosses Multi lands, that week’s Punters Club players receive a next-day Poker freeroll for the full amount (details in Promo Terms).

  • Tournament format: No-limit hold’em with blind levels.
  • Community first: Friendly table, competitive mindset.
  • Consistent structure: Same rules each week.

From Melbourne poker tournament regulars to Sydney cash-game grinders trying structured events, Big Bluff Bay is built for Australian players who want consistency and value (start with Poker in Australia and current schedule).
