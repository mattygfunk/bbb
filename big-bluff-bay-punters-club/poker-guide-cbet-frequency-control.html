Poker Guide: C-Bet Frequency Control

Board-texture and range-advantage method to control continuation-bet frequency for better EV and less autopilot.

Pair with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Frequency by board class

One c-bet frequency for all boards is a leak.

  • High-card dry boards: c-bet often, small sizing.
  • Low connected boards: check more, protect checking range.
  • Monotone/dynamic boards: use selective bets with blockers.

Sizing tied to purpose

Bet size should match target range behavior.

  • Small bets to deny equity and realize range edge.
  • Larger bets when polar and targeting capped continues.
  • Avoid medium-size autopilot that does neither well.

Second-barrel eligibility

Flop bet quality is measured on turn readiness.

  • Bet flop only when enough turn cards continue pressure.
  • Check back flop more with weak barrel futures.
  • Track spots where flop c-bet forced awkward turn decisions.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag five spots.
  • Week 2: Review and define one correction rule.
  • Week 3: Stress-test rule under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on outcomes.
