What to play in Gold Coast (2-track approach)

Bankroll framework for Gold Coast players

  • Set a weekly bankroll cap before the week begins.
  • Split budget by purpose (e.g. 60% online volume / 40% live adaptation).
  • Use stop-loss and session-time limits to control fatigue-driven mistakes.
  • Never increase buy-in level just to recover previous losses.

Weekly improvement loop (simple and effective)

  1. Plan session windows before the week starts.
  2. Play your scheduled events without impulse add-ons.
  3. Log 3 mistakes and 3 good decisions after each session.
  4. Adjust next week’s game selection based on those notes.

This loop compounds quickly, especially for players balancing local Gold Coast sessions with online tournament volume.
