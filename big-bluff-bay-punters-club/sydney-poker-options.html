Live pathways around Sydney

Always verify current details directly (buy-ins, start times, and structure can change). Typical live pathways include:

Practical advice: online vs live split

  • Development phase: 70% online / 30% live to build hand-volume and core spots fast
  • Performance phase: 50/50 split once your fundamentals are stable
  • Bankroll discipline: pre-set weekly stop-loss and session time caps
  • Review loop: track mistakes after every session and adjust next-week game selection
