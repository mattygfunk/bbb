Poker Guide: Population Reads Online

Use population tendencies to simplify decisions and exploit common pool leaks in online tournaments.

Use with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Build population priors

  • Map default fold-to-cbet, turn over-fold, and river call rates.
  • Segment by stake/time/format for cleaner reads.
  • Start exploit from pool baseline, then adjust to villain.

Exploit templates

  • Versus over-fold pools: increase pressure lines.
  • Versus sticky pools: shift to value-heavy betting.
  • Use blocker-aware bluffs only where folds exist.

Review and calibration

  • Track exploit outcomes by node type.
  • Remove exploits that don’t print over sample.
  • Update priors monthly, not emotionally after one session.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag 5 spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction.
  • Week 3: Stress-test correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on data.
