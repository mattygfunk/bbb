5-star weekly plan (simple template)

  1. Lock your primary event before the week starts (usually Sunday Main).
  2. Add 2–4 nightly events based on time/energy, not impulse.
  3. Set bankroll limits before first buy-in.
  4. Review one key mistake and one strong decision after each session.
