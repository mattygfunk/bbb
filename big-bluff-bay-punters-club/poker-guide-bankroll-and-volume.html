Poker Guide: Bankroll + Volume Framework

Apply this guide with your weekly event plan for better consistency and stronger decision quality.

Framework goal

Use one weekly system that protects bankroll, improves decision quality, and compounds over time.

Step 1: Build a weekly bankroll envelope

  • Set one fixed weekly bankroll before first event.
  • Split into core events, optional events, and buffer.
  • Never borrow from next week to chase losses.

Step 2: Plan volume, then protect quality

  • Pick anchor events from Poker Schedule.
  • Add optional events only when focus is strong.
  • Drop low-quality late sessions when fatigue appears.

Step 3: Review loop that compounds

  1. Log one strong decision and one costly mistake per session.
  2. Classify errors as technical vs emotional.
  3. Apply one targeted adjustment next session.

Common leaks this prevents

  • Overplaying due to FOMO.
  • Emotional add-ons after losses.
  • No post-session review.
