Poker Guide: Bankroll + Volume Framework

Most players underperform because they mix too many events with no financial structure. This guide gives you a simple weekly operating model: set limits, select events on purpose, and review decisions with consistency.

Step 1: Build a weekly bankroll envelope

  • Set one fixed weekly bankroll before the week starts.
  • Split it into core events, optional events, and buffer.
  • Never borrow from next week’s envelope to “recover”.
  • If you hit your weekly cap, stop and review instead of forcing action.

Step 2: Plan volume, then protect quality

Use the Poker Schedule to choose your anchor events first. Add extras only if focus and energy are still high. Quality decisions beat inflated volume over time.

  • Anchor example: Sunday Main + two nightly windows.
  • Add-ons should be conditional, not automatic.
  • Cut low-quality late sessions if they hurt decision quality.

Step 3: Use a review loop that compounds

  1. After each session, log one strong decision and one costly mistake.
  2. Classify mistakes: technical (range/spot) or emotional (tilt/fatigue/FOMO).
  3. Apply one change next session only (avoid over-correcting everything at once).

Over 4–8 weeks, this is where edge compounds.

Common leaks this framework prevents

  • Late-session punt entries with no edge.
  • Chasing losses with higher buy-ins.
  • No post-session review, so mistakes repeat weekly.
  • Too many events, not enough focus.
