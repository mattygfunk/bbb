🎯 Scoring System

Basic Scoring

Each correct tip = 1 point

Perfect Round Bonus

Get all tips correct in a round = +1 bonus point

Example: 9 correct tips + perfect bonus = 10 points total

Double Points Multiplier

Activate Double Points = round score × 2

Example: 7 correct tips with Double Points = 14 points

Tiebreaker

Margin prediction on first game of each round

Closest to actual winning margin breaks ties in weekly and season standings

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my tips after submitting?

Yes, until each individual game kicks off. After kickoff, that game's tip is locked.

What happens if I join mid-season?

You get your Double Points activation immediately and compete from that round onwards. No penalty for late joining.

Can I use Double Points on a round where I've already tipped?

Yes, as long as the first game hasn't kicked off yet. You can activate/deactivate freely before round lockout.

How does the margin tiebreaker work?

You predict the winning margin of the first game each round. If players tie on points, closest margin prediction wins.

What if I don't submit a margin prediction?

You'll be treated as predicting a 0-point margin for tiebreaker purposes.

Do byes affect perfect round bonus?

Perfect round bonus only applies to games with results. Bye rounds are excluded from perfect round calculations.
