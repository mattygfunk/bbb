Entry & Payment

$75 season entry · Cash only · BSB bank transfer
  • Entry fee is $75 for the full 2026 AFL season.
  • Payment is by BSB bank transfer only — no PayPal, no card, no cash in hand.
  • After you click Register My Entry, payment instructions (BSB + account number) will be shown.
  • Use your BBB username as the payment reference.
  • Your entry remains Pending until the admin confirms payment. You cannot submit tips until confirmed.
  • Entries close before Round 1 first-game lockout. Late entries may be accepted at admin discretion but are not guaranteed.

⚠️ If your payment is not received within 48 hours of registering, your entry may be cancelled. Contact the organiser if you have payment issues.

Tipping Rules

  • Each round you tip the winner of every AFL match.
  • Individual game lockouts — each game locks at its scheduled kickoff time (Melbourne time, AEST/AEDT). Once locked, that tip cannot be changed.
  • You may edit tips for unlocked games at any time before their individual kickoff.
  • Tips not submitted before a game's kickoff default to no tip for that game (0 points).
  • Draws: In the rare case of a draw, no one scores a point for that game.
  • The "Quick Pick" button selects the home team for all unlocked games as a starting point — you can still change any pick.

Scoring

1 point per correct tip. Simple.
  • 1 point for each correctly tipped winner.
  • Maximum possible score = number of games in the round (typically 9).
  • Season ladder = cumulative total points across all rounds.
  • Missed rounds count as 0 points for that round.

Tie-Breaker Rules

If two or more players are tied on season points at the end of the season:

  1. Most rounds with the highest score (most "perfect rounds" or "joint-high rounds")
  2. Most total games correctly tipped across all rounds (same as season points — this would still be tied)
  3. Most consecutive rounds tipped without missing a game
  4. If still tied: prize is split equally between tied players

Weekly Jackpot

$30 base weekly jackpot. Rolls over if tied.
  • Each round has a $30 weekly jackpot.
  • The player with the highest number of correct tips for that round wins the jackpot outright.
  • If two or more players tie for the weekly high score ROLLOVER — the jackpot rolls over to the following round.
  • Rollovers are cumulative — if multiple weeks roll over, the jackpot grows.
  • The jackpot is paid out in cash to the weekly winner.
  • Players with a confirmed (paid) entry only are eligible for the weekly jackpot.
  • Current jackpot state is displayed on the hub page and leaderboard.

FAQ

How do I pay?

Register your entry on the hub page. You'll see BSB bank transfer details. Transfer exactly $75 with your username as the reference. The admin will confirm your entry within 24–48 hours.

Can I still tip if my payment is pending?

No. Tips require a confirmed (paid) entry. Your entry status shows on the hub page once logged in.

What if I miss a round?

Missing a round scores 0 points for that round. Your season total is not affected beyond that — you're still in the competition.

What if a game is postponed or abandoned?

Postponed/abandoned games are excluded from scoring for that round. Your tip for that game is ignored and doesn't count for or against you.

When are weekly jackpots paid?

Weekly jackpots are paid via bank transfer after results are confirmed and settled, typically within 48 hours of the last game of the round.

What happens if there's a tie for the weekly jackpot every week?

The jackpot keeps rolling over and accumulating. There's no limit to how large it can grow. The jackpot is only paid when there is a single outright weekly high scorer.

What's the "margin tip" / banker game?

Not implemented in the 2026 season. All games use the simple correct/incorrect scoring model.

Can I enter mid-season?

Entries are intended to close before Round 1. Mid-season entries may be accepted at admin discretion but will start from 0 points and cannot retroactively tip completed rounds.

Is this gambling?

Big Bluff Bay AFL Tipping is a private social club competition between members. It is not operated by a licensed bookmaker. Participation is by invitation/registration only.
