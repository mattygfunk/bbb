Poker Guide: Postflop Plan By Street

Build a clear flop-turn-river decision map: c-bet strategy, turn barreling logic, and river value/bluff plans for tournament poker.

Use this with Poker FAQ and Poker Rules.

Street 1: Flop plan

Choose c-bet frequency and size from board texture and range advantage, not autopilot.

  • Dry/high-card boards: small c-bet at higher frequency.
  • Connected/two-tone boards: check more range, bet stronger value/draws.
  • Before betting flop, identify good and bad turn cards.

Street 2: Turn plan

Turn is where stacks get pressured. Decide between pressure, control, or give-up lines.

  • Barrel scare cards that improve your perceived range.
  • Check medium-strength hands that prefer showdown realization.
  • Use larger sizes when targeting capped one-pair ranges.

Street 3: River plan

River should be target-based: what calls value, what folds to bluff.

  • Value bet only if worse hands can call enough.
  • Bluff when blockers are good and population folds enough.
  • Avoid thin hero bluffs versus sticky player pools.

30-day implementation plan

  • Week 1: Apply this guide in two sessions and tag 5 relevant spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction rule.
  • Week 3: Pressure-test the correction in live play.
  • Week 4: Keep, refine, or replace based on outcomes.
