Poker Guide: Postflop Plan By Street

Most tournament leaks happen when players play each street in isolation. Strong players plan ahead: flop action sets up turn options, and turn action sets up river clarity.

Use this with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Street 1: Flop plan (what story are you telling?)

  • Range advantage boards (A-high, dry): c-bet small and frequently.
  • Dynamic boards (connected/two-tone): c-bet less, check more range.
  • Multi-way pots: tighten value threshold; bluff much less.
  • Before betting, decide: Which turns help me barrel? Which turns make me shut down?

If you can’t name your good and bad turn cards before betting flop, your flop bet is probably too automatic.

Street 2: Turn plan (pressure or control)

  • Barrel turns that improve your perceived range (overcards, scare cards, flush completers when you have blockers).
  • Check back turns when equity is thin and river realization matters.
  • Size up on turn when targeting capped one-pair ranges.
  • With medium strength, choose pot control lines that preserve showdown value.

Turn is where pots get big. Decide whether this hand is becoming a 2-street value hand, 2-street bluff, or check-to-showdown line.

Street 3: River plan (value target or bluff target)

  • Value bet when worse hands can realistically call.
  • Bluff only when you block strong calls and unblock folds.
  • Don’t hero-bluff into population that over-calls river.
  • Don’t auto-check strong bluff-catchers if thin value exists.

River decisions should be binary: what exactly calls me if I value bet, and what exactly folds if I bluff?

Quick in-hand checklist

  1. What is villain’s range after preflop action?
  2. Who has range/nut advantage on this flop?
  3. If I bet flop, which turn cards continue my line?
  4. If called on turn, what river cards are value / bluff / give-up?

Most common postflop leaks

  • Flop c-betting without a turn plan.
  • Turn barreling because “I already bet flop”.
  • River betting with no clear target hand class.
  • Using one size for everything regardless of board texture.

30-day improvement plan

  • Week 1: Tag 10 postflop hands where you reached turn.
  • Week 2: Label each: no plan / good flop plan / good full-street plan.
  • Week 3: Force yourself to verbalize turn and river plan before flop c-bet.
  • Week 4: Review EV impact with Session Review Scorecard.
