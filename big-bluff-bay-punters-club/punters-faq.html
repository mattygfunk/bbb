Punters Club FAQ

1) How are points scored each race?

3 points for 1st, 2 for 2nd, and 1 for 3rd.

2) When do tips lock?

Tips lock at the published lock timer and cannot be edited after lock.

3) Can I edit my tips after submitting?

Yes, any time before lock.

4) Do I need an account to tip?

Yes, register and log in to submit tips.

5) Can I submit fewer than all races?

Yes, you can tip selected races only.

6) How is the weekly winner decided?

Highest points wins; tie-break uses winners count as configured.

7) What is a winning pick?

A selection that finishes first in a race result.

8) What does the Winners column mean?

How many first-place picks a member has landed.

9) How does jackpot rollover work?

If there is no outright weekly winner, jackpot carries to next week.

10) How is current jackpot calculated?

Paid entries x entry fee + any rollover balance.

11) What is the entry fee?

Current default is $20 per player.

12) Does unpaid status affect jackpot eligibility?

Yes, payout eligibility can require paid status for the round.

13) What happens if race results are corrected?

Admins can update results and the ladder recalculates.

14) Can admins edit tips?

Yes, admins can correct status, paid flag, race, and selection.

15) How does substitution handling work?

If your runner is scratched and mapped to a substitute, points apply to the substitute.

16) Can new members join any week?

Yes, subject to registration and club rules.

17) Can I view all submitted tips?

Yes, community tips are visible on the Punters page.

18) How often does leaderboard refresh?

The page auto-refreshes regularly for live updates.

19) Can I see race-by-race points?

Yes, tap a member name in the leaderboard.

20) How do streak milestones work?

Consecutive active weeks build current streak and best streak.

21) Which streak badges exist?

Streak 4, 8, 12, 26, and 52 weeks.

22) Which win badges exist?

Weekly Winner, Back-to-Back, and 3/5/10-Time Winner.

23) Where can I see winner history?

In Winners Hub and milestone sections.

24) Can I share the leaderboard?

Yes, use the built-in share button.

25) What is Bosses Bet?

A tracked multi used for bonus promotions.

26) What if Bosses Multi lands?

Eligible weekly players receive a next-day poker freeroll bonus.

27) Where do I find upcoming fixtures?

At the top of the Punters page and on Home fixtures section.

28) Can I reset tips for a new round?

Admins archive rounds and start a fresh week state.

29) How are disputes handled?

Contact admin; updates and corrections are logged by admin actions.

30) Is there a code of conduct?

Yes: respectful behaviour, fair play, and no abuse.

31) Can I use mobile?

Yes, the site is mobile-friendly for tips and tracking.

32) What browser works best?

Latest Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.

33) Where are policy pages?

See Terms and Privacy in the footer.

34) How do I contact admin?

Use the admin contact link on club pages.

35) Can I play poker too?

Yes, use the Poker page schedule and join flow.

36) What is the Sunday poker event?

Weekly Sunday 6 PM $1,300 GTD Main Event.

37) What nightly poker events run?

8 PM $500 GTD + ticket, and 10 PM $700 GTD.

38) Can punters become poker regulars?

Yes, that is a core Big Bluff Bay pathway.

39) How often are FAQs updated?

As rules, schedules, and promotions evolve.

40) What should I do before lock?

Submit tips early, confirm picks, and ensure paid status if required.
