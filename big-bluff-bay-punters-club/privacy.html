Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 February 2026

Big Bluff Bay ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you use the club site, we may store your username, encrypted password hash, race tips, paid/unpaid status, leaderboard points, and related timestamps. If you contact us through social channels, those platforms may also process your data under their own policies.

How we use data

We use data to run club features: member login, tip submission, score calculation, jackpot and leaderboard display, and admin moderation.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be visible to other members where required for club operation (for example, leaderboard names and tips pages).

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data, including secure transport (HTTPS), access controls for admin actions, and password hashing.

Data retention

We keep operational records as long as needed for running the club and resolving disputes. You can request account deletion by contacting the club admin.

Your choices

You can request to update or remove your account details. Club gameplay records may be retained for integrity and audit purposes.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the Big Bluff Bay admin through the official Facebook or Instagram pages linked in the site footer.
