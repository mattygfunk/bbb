Poker Guide: Table Selection and Seat Dynamics

Use this guide with your weekly plan from Poker Schedule and policy clarity from Poker FAQ.

Why table selection matters

  • Game quality often beats marginal strategy edges.
  • Seat position against aggro regs affects your EV heavily.
  • Fatigue + bad table choice is a costly combo.

Seat dynamics checklist

  1. Map who opens too wide.
  2. Track who over-folds to pressure.
  3. Adjust seat and event choices weekly.
