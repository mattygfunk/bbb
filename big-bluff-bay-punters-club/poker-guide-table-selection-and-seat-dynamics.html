Poker Guide: Table Selection And Seat Dynamics

Improve EV before cards are dealt through better table selection and seat positioning decisions.

Use with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Table selection edge

  • Select softer lineups over bigger prize vanity spots.
  • Avoid reg-dense pools if your edge is marginal there.
  • Reassess table quality after major bust-outs/re-entries.

Seat dynamics

  • Prioritize position on aggressive regulars.
  • Keep weaker players on your right when possible.
  • Adjust opening frequencies based on blind tendencies.

Execution checklist

  • Rate table quality every 30–45 minutes.
  • Move or re-register when edge declines materially.
  • Track ROI by table type to validate instincts.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag 5 spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction.
  • Week 3: Stress-test correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on data.
