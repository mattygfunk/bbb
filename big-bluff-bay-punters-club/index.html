Punters Club Guide: Scoring, Bankroll, and Weekly Edge

Big Bluff Bay is built for disciplined players, not random guesswork. Each week you submit your runners before lock, then track progress through the live leaderboard as official race results are posted. Scoring is straightforward: 3 points for first, 2 for second, and 1 for third (full breakdown in the Punters FAQ). Over a season, that creates pressure to make high-quality picks consistently rather than swinging for miracle outcomes every race.

The weekly format rewards structure. Strong punters often focus on races where they have a genuine read on pace, map, and price value, then avoid forcing low-confidence picks. That approach protects bankroll mindset and improves long-run results. You can still chase upside through the jackpot mechanic, but sustainable success usually comes from smart race selection and repeatable process (see How Punters Club Works).

Our leaderboard now tracks both total points and winners, so members can see who is converting top selections — not just collecting place points. This makes competition clearer and keeps the season engaging for everyone in the club. If there is a tie at the top for a round, jackpot rollover rules remain in play as published in the Punters Rules. If the Bosses Multi lands, eligible weekly players also receive the poker freeroll bonus (details in Promo Terms).

If you’re new, start simple: lock your entries early, avoid late rushed edits, and prioritize races you understand best. Over time, consistency beats hero plays. That is the core club philosophy: back sharp, play patient, and win like a club. When you’re ready, convert punting discipline into cards through our poker tournament schedule and Poker FAQ.
