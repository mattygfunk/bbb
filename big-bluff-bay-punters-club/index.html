Rules + FAQ

When do picks lock?

At the published lock timer in the status strip.

How is scoring calculated?

3 points for 1st, 2 for 2nd, 1 for 3rd.

Can I edit tips after submitting?

Yes, until lock time. After lock, tips are frozen.

How does paid status affect jackpot?

Current pot is calculated from paid players for that week.

What is the Bosses Multi freeroll bonus?

If Bosses Multi lands, that week’s Punters Club players get a next-day Poker freeroll for the full amount.
