Poker Guide: Final Table Adjustments

Final-table playbook for pay-jump pressure, stack leverage, and risk-adjusted aggression.

Pair with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

ICM first, ego second

Final tables are payout problems before they are chip-EV problems.

  • When medium stacked, avoid marginal stack-offs versus covering stacks.
  • When covering others, pressure ladder-sensitive players aggressively.
  • Rebuild open/call/jam ranges each pay jump.

Stack leverage map

Your line should reflect relative stack pressure, not absolute chips.

  • Big stack: attack medium stacks protecting pay jumps.
  • Medium stack: defend stack utility; avoid thin hero calls.
  • Short stack: identify high fold-equity reshove spots early.

Endgame execution

Small mistakes at final table cost the most money.

  • Pre-plan tough nodes before action reaches you.
  • Use time-bank on close ICM nodes, not routine folds.
  • Tag final-table hands for immediate next-day review.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag five spots.
  • Week 2: Review and define one correction rule.
  • Week 3: Stress-test rule under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on outcomes.
