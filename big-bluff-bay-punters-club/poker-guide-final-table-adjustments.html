Poker Guide: Final Table Adjustments

Apply this guide with your weekly event plan for better consistency and stronger decision quality.

Final-table mindset

Final tables are pressure environments. Prioritise payout-aware decisions over ego plays.

ICM-aware aggression

  • Attack medium stacks when you cover them.
  • Steal wider versus ladder-sensitive short stacks.
  • Reduce thin calls when payout pressure is high.

Stack-depth adjustments

  • Short: maximise fold equity and push spots.
  • Medium: avoid marginal collisions with covering stacks.
  • Big: apply measured pressure, not random chaos.

Heads-up transition

Widen ranges, value-bet thinner, and adapt quickly to opponent tempo.

Review rubric

  1. Did you respect pay-jump pressure?
  2. Were your steals timed correctly?
  3. Where did you over-defend?
