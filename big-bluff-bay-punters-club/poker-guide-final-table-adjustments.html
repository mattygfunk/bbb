Poker Guide: Final Table Adjustments

Practical final-table adjustments: pay jumps, ICM pressure, stack-depth decisions, and aggression timing.

Core final-table adjustments

  • Widen steals vs short stacks that are ladder-sensitive.
  • Reduce marginal calls where pay-jump risk is high.
  • Pressure medium stacks when your stack covers them.
  • Use position to control pot size with fragile holdings.

Execution note

Final-table edge is mostly discipline + pressure timing, not hero calls.
