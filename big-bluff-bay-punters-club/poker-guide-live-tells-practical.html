Poker Guide: Live Tells Practical

Practical live-tells framework that integrates physical reads with betting-line evidence for better decisions.

Use with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Reliable tell categories

  • Baseline deviations matter more than generic tells.
  • Timing shifts + size shifts together are strongest.
  • Hands/shaking/breathing cues are supporting evidence only.

Tell weighting model

  • Primary: range + line consistency.
  • Secondary: physical tell that confirms or questions line.
  • Never override clear betting logic with one weak tell.

Common tell traps

  • Projecting emotion instead of observing baselines.
  • Over-valuing one dramatic motion.
  • Ignoring player type and stakes context.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag 5 spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction.
  • Week 3: Stress-test correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on data.
