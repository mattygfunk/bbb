Poker Guide: Heads Up Transition Play

Transition smoothly from final-table play to heads-up with clear opening, defending, and aggression adjustments.

Use with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Immediate transition priorities

  • Reset mentally: HU is range-war, not ladder survival.
  • Open frequency rises dramatically by position.
  • Adapt quickly to opponent’s defend and 3-bet profile.

Core HU structure

  • Mix small raises and limps based on opponent response.
  • Attack capped lines on turn and river.
  • Protect check-back range to avoid predictable barrels.

Adjustment loop

  • Tag first 20 HU hands for leak detection.
  • Exploit repeated over-fold/over-call tendencies fast.
  • Stabilize emotional swings after momentum shifts.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag 5 spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction.
  • Week 3: Stress-test correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on data.
