Poker Rules (Universal Standards + House Rules)

This page is written to be clear, fair, and practical. It follows widely accepted tournament principles used across poker rooms and league play. Where house policy differs for a specific event, the posted event conditions apply for that session.

Core principle: protect game integrity, keep action moving, and resolve disputes consistently.

1) Universal Fundamentals

  • One player to a hand: no outside advice, signaling, or assistance.
  • Cards speak: the actual hand value at showdown determines outcome.
  • Act in turn: acting out of turn may be binding if action does not change.
  • Verbal is binding: clear declarations like “call”, “raise”, “all-in”, or stated amounts are binding.
  • Ethical duty: players must avoid collusion, soft play, chip dumping, or angle-shooting.

2) Betting & Raise Rules

  • Minimum raise size: a legal raise must be at least the size of the previous full raise.
  • Single oversized chip: placing one oversized chip without saying “raise” is a call.
  • String bets prohibited: betting/raising must be in one clear motion or one declaration.
  • All-in exceptions: short all-ins may not reopen action unless they meet reopen thresholds.
  • Call amount clarity: players are responsible for determining call amount before acting.

3) Preflop, Blinds, and Dealing

  • Dealer button and blinds move each hand according to tournament flow.
  • Players must post blinds/antes as required by level structure.
  • Substantial action may lock a hand state even if a minor dealing error is discovered.
  • Floor/admin can declare a misdeal before substantial action if dealing was materially incorrect.

4) Showdown Rules

  • At showdown, players must table cards to win chips in contested pots.
  • Last aggressor on final betting round generally shows first; otherwise normal order applies.
  • Mucked hands are dead once irretrievable and clearly released.
  • Ties split according to hand rankings and pot-splitting rules.

5) Player Conduct & Integrity

  • No collusion: any coordinated play or information sharing is prohibited.
  • No abusive behaviour: harassment, threats, and repeated toxic chat are penalties.
  • No stalling abuse: unreasonable delay may receive warning/penalty.
  • Respect action order: deliberate disruption of action flow is penalized.

6) Penalties & Enforcement

Penalties escalate by severity and repetition. Typical progression:

  • Verbal warning
  • Timed penalty / missed hands
  • Chip penalty (where event policy allows)
  • Disqualification for serious or repeated integrity breaches

Serious integrity violations (collusion, chip dumping, abusive conduct, deliberate cheating) can bypass warnings and result in immediate removal.

7) Disputes, Rulings, and Final Authority

  • If a dispute occurs, stop action and call admin/floor immediately.
  • Best available evidence (action history, table reports, sequence) is used.
  • Admin/floor rulings are final for that event.
  • Post-event policy feedback is welcome via club channels and FAQ updates.
