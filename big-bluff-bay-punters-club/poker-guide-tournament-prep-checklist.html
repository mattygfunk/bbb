Poker Guide: Tournament Prep Checklist

A pre-session checklist for Big Bluff Bay players: setup, focus, bankroll, and game selection before first hand.

Pre-session checklist

  • Confirm your event slate from Poker Schedule.
  • Set bankroll cap and stop-loss boundary.
  • Remove distractions and prepare note template.
  • Define one focus objective (e.g. 3-bet pots).

During session

Prioritise decision quality over volume. If focus drops, cut optional events.
