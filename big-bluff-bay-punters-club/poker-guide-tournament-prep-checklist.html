Poker Guide: Tournament Prep Checklist

A complete pre-tournament checklist covering technical prep, mental readiness, and logistical reliability.

Pair with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Technical prep

Remove avoidable decision friction before start.

  • Review opening and reshove charts for today’s stack profiles.
  • Preload notes on frequent opponents and table tendencies.
  • Set default plans for bubble/final-table pressure nodes.

Mental prep

Start calm, focused, and process-oriented.

  • Define one session objective you can control.
  • Commit to A-game standards (pace, patience, discipline).
  • Visualize response to bad beats before they happen.

Logistics prep

Operational errors are silent bankroll leaks.

  • Confirm schedule, buy-ins, and break windows.
  • Eliminate device/network distractions for online sessions.
  • Prepare nutrition/hydration so focus does not crash late.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag five spots.
  • Week 2: Review and define one correction rule.
  • Week 3: Stress-test rule under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on outcomes.
