General Poker FAQs

What game format is used?

No-limit Texas Hold’em tournament format with structured blind levels.

Is this cash game or tournament play?

Tournament play, not an open-ended cash table.

Can beginners play?

Absolutely. New players are welcome if they follow posted structure and table etiquette.

Can I join late or re-enter?

Late reg and re-entry depend on that event’s posted conditions.

What does GTD mean?

Guaranteed Prize Pool — the minimum prize amount for that event.

Are payouts winner-take-all?

No. Payouts are distributed by the event’s payout structure.

Are collusion and chip-dumping allowed?

Never. Any collusion, soft play, or chip-dumping can lead to disqualification.

Who handles disputes or rulings?

Admin rulings can be reviewed with hand/timing context where available.

Can I play on mobile?

Site information is mobile-friendly; gameplay format details are posted per event.

What should I do before my first event?

Register early, confirm seat, read rules, and arrive before start time.
