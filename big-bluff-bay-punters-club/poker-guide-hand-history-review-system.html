Poker Guide: Hand History Review System

Use this guide with your weekly plan from Poker Schedule and policy clarity from Poker FAQ.

Weekly review system

  • Collect 10 hands: 4 wins, 4 losses, 2 uncertain spots.
  • Classify: technical error, emotional error, variance-only.
  • Pick one leak to fix next week.

Output rule

Each review must produce one actionable adjustment, not five vague ideas.
