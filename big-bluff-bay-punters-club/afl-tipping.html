❓ Double Points FAQ
When do I receive Double Points?

At season start (Round 1), and when you join mid-season — you get 1 activation per season.

Can I use both in the same round?

No — one activation per round. Each activation doubles that round's total score.

Can I undo it?

Yes — you can toggle it off before lockout. After lockout it's locked in and consumed.

Do I lose them if I don't use them?

Unused activations expire at the end of the season.

📖 How AFL Tipping Works

🎯 Scoring System

  • 1 point per correct tip
  • +1 bonus point for perfect round (all tips correct)
  • Double Points multiplies your round score by 2

⚡ Double Points Rules

  • 1 activation per season (changed from 2)
  • Activate before round lockout
  • Can toggle off before lockout
  • Expires at season end if unused

🏁 Tiebreaker

  • Margin prediction on first game of round
  • Closest to actual margin wins ties
  • Submitted with your tips

🔒 Lock Rules

  • Individual game lockouts at kickoff
  • No tips or changes after lockout
  • Double Points locks with round
