Poker Guide: Common Mistakes Australian Players Make

Apply this guide with your weekly event plan for better consistency and stronger decision quality.

Top recurring leaks

  • Playing too many events with no plan
  • Over-defending weak holdings late
  • Ignoring fatigue effects on decision quality

Leak 1: Unplanned volume

Fix: pre-commit event slate using Poker Schedule and reject impulse entries.

Leak 2: Emotional bankroll management

Fix: hard weekly cap + no escalation after losing sessions.

Leak 3: No review loop

Fix: log one technical and one mental leak each session.

30-day fix plan

  1. Pick one leak weekly.
  2. Track in 3 sessions.
  3. Keep or replace intervention based on outcomes.
