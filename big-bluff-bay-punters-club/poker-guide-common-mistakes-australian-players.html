Poker Guide: Common Mistakes Australian Players Make

Most common recurring leaks for Australian poker players and how to fix them quickly.

Most common leaks

  • Playing too many events without a plan
  • Overvaluing weak top pair in bloated pots
  • Failing to adjust by stack depth late
  • No post-session review routine

Fast fix

Pick one leak per week and track whether it improved after 3 sessions.
