Poker Guide: Turn And River Discipline

Turn/river discipline system to prevent expensive spew and improve value capture in high-leverage streets.

Use with Poker Schedule, Poker FAQ, and Poker Rules.

Turn decision discipline

  • Classify hand: value, bluff, showdown, give-up.
  • Size to objective, not fear.
  • Avoid “because I bet flop” autopilot barrels.

River clarity

  • Define exact value target before betting.
  • Bluff where blockers and fold rates align.
  • Check when neither value target nor fold target is clear.

High-cost leaks

  • Thin hero calls versus under-bluffed pools.
  • River bluffs into call-happy population.
  • Missing thin value from fear of being raised.

30-day implementation

  • Week 1: Apply in two sessions and tag 5 spots.
  • Week 2: Review tags and define one correction.
  • Week 3: Stress-test correction under pressure.
  • Week 4: Keep/refine/replace based on data.
