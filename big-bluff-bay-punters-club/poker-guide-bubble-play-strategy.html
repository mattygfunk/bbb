Poker Guide: Bubble Play Strategy

Use this guide with your weekly plan from Poker Schedule and policy clarity from Poker FAQ.

Core bubble principles

  • Pressure stacks that can’t bust comfortably.
  • Avoid thin hero calls versus covering stacks.
  • Steal more in position, defend tighter out of position.

Execution checklist

  1. Identify vulnerable stacks each orbit.
  2. Tag high-ICM risk spots before acting.
  3. Choose low-variance edges over ego bluffs.
