Promotion Terms (Official)

These terms apply to promotional components on Big Bluff Bay pages, including guarantees (GTD), ticket pathways, freerolls, and major-event prize references. Event-specific cards may add conditions that override general wording for that event.

1) GTD (Guaranteed Prize Pool) Terms

  • Displayed GTD values are minimum guaranteed prize pools for listed events.
  • Prize pools may increase above GTD based on event settings and participation.
  • If an event card is corrected, the corrected listing becomes authoritative.

2) Ticket Pathway Terms

  • “+ Main Event Ticket” references follow the posted eligibility conditions for that session.
  • Ticket transferability, stacking, expiry, and redemption timing are event-specific.
  • Where unclear, published admin clarification applies for that event.

3) Freeroll Terms

  • Freerolls are promotional events with eligibility requirements.
  • Freeroll value or conditions may depend on source event outcomes (e.g. promo triggers).
  • Freeroll entries are not cash equivalents unless explicitly stated.

4) Major-Event Prize Terms

  • References to major prizes (e.g. holiday packages) are examples unless event card confirms live offer.
  • Prize fulfilment conditions, timelines, and substitutions (if any) are event-specific.
  • Any third-party supplier terms apply where prizes involve external providers.

5) Fairness, Eligibility, and Amendments

  • Promotions may require compliance with published rules and conduct standards.
  • Big Bluff Bay may amend, suspend, or cancel promotions for fairness, integrity, compliance, or operational reasons.
  • In disputes, best available event data and posted conditions determine outcome.
